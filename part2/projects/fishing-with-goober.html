About This Project

Made for Tritonware Spring '25, Fishing With Goober is a physics-based game where you control your boat with your little fish body and catch the fish flying out of the water around you with your trusty net. Keep the evil cat satisfied with regular feeding or face his mighty wrath!

  • Role: I was in charge of programming the UI elements and background of the game, bringing the amazing art to life.
  • Tools: Godot
