About This Project

Bedtime Beats is a game where you control a child trying to finish the last rhythm game level before going to bed, but must keep his ears close to the ground for his parent's footstep, as who knows what happens if he's caught beyond bedtime.

  • Role: I worked on the rhythm game logic, both the logic of the notes, the score system as well as the life functions.
  • Tools: Unity
